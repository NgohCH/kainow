# Automated accessibility pass

`audit.mjs` runs axe-core (WCAG 2.0/2.1/2.2 A and AA rules, plus best-practice rules) in headless Chrome over every screen, at 1200 px and 375 px wide: sign-in, the code step, the wrong-code error, an invitation while signed out, My Projects (and the new-project form), a project's overview (and the defer form and department move), people (with a held member, a grant request, the finding, handover and grant forms) and notes, a Closed project's archive panel, a handover invitation while signed out, a note, the review screen, the wording editor, administrators, departments, domains, AI (models, limits and policy, usage), and (sub-project 2b) the Approvals settings, the Work tab with change requests waiting (0 of 2 and 1 of 2 approvals) and a decision replaced with its review flags, the amend, cancel, decision-replacement, flag-clearing and request-rejection forms, the Closing screen not ready and ready (with the disposition form open and delegation), a deferred project's Deadline review (a request waiting, new due dates, and the finished-during-the-pause form) and the Overview of those projects, and (sub-project 11a, the interface redesign) Home in the Red and Teal schemes and the Dark theme, My tasks, Reviews, Projects, the Settings hub and each of its 18 setting pages, the person menu open, the side menu collapsed and opened at 375 px, a project's header and binder tabs, its Discussion, Notes, Proposals and Settings tabs (with the Stages box), the review screen inside the project with a Change history and with the correction panel open, the organisation colour form with a colour it refuses, the Stage templates page with a template open, the new-project form with a type of project chosen, and a project with a 120-character name. The audit sets the organisation's approval count to 2 and lets projects override it, and adds a second Sponsor account directly in the database. It also makes the administrator a designated security reviewer (so the hub shows all 18 settings), corrects one AI proposal's type through the API, and fails any state whose page scrolls sideways (`page-scrolls-sideways`, counted as a violation).

**The 11a.1 walk** (`walkthrough.mjs`, D133 to D141) runs first, on projects it makes itself, with the server's real replies: it does on screen what a person would do, checks what the screens then say (the audit stops with the sentence that was not there), and audits each state at both widths:

- the task page in every status, with Start, Block, Unblock and Complete pressed by its assignee, and its forms; the old `#/projects/<id>/work/<task>` address;
- proposal, source and record links followed round by clicks: a person's own proposal, a note's run (its passage marked in the note) and a discussion's run (its message), each with a task and a decision authorised on the review screen;
- the resource catalogue empty, its first item with a new category in the same form, and after;
- a stage renamed (and a name already used refused);
- a project deferred, resumed, deferred, closed, reopened and closed while Active: after each, the header, stage flow, History, Timeline bars, Projects (Current projects and History) and Home;
- Home's Needs you by project, and My tasks as a board, by project (a box closed, all rows shown) and by due date;
- My profile with a past role, a retired department, its forms, and the tab style chosen there;
- Duplicate project untouched, with old terms marked, with all confirmed, refused (a text like a credential; a case opened meanwhile) and created; its proposals as seen by someone who may not open the original; Authorise several with what is missing named, then accepted; the copied line on a credential case;
- the three tab styles, Light and Dark, and the new pages in Dark.

**The boards and the calmer look (sub-project 11a.2, D142 and D143)** are audited after the 11a screens, at both widths, in Light and in Dark: the Work tab's board by status with Completed folded and then unfolded (a task completed today, and a Blocked one whose reason must show on its box), the side panel open and with a form open in it, the board by assignee, the list view; My tasks as a board, by project and by due date; Home's Needs you as a board and by project; then the dark side menu, compact spacing, and both together in Dark (Home with the side menu opened below 900 px, the Work board, My tasks and the side panel). A keyboard-only check follows at each width: Tab to a box, Enter opens its side panel (focus on its heading), Tab to Start and Enter, Escape closes the panel, and focus must be back on the box, now in In progress. The board shows a parent box whose subtasks (one overdue, one Blocked with a reason) must keep their labels, date and reason inside it. The run measures the box and column edges against what is beside them, in Red and in Teal, Light and Dark ("Board edges" at the end), and counts any edge below 3:1 as a violation (D143). The row forms of the Work tab (Complete, History, Propose a change and others) are audited in the list view.

What cannot be done without an AI provider is written to the database as Kainow writes it: the finished AI runs, the discussion's transcript and its places. Memberships are added in the database too.

**The audit fails (exit code 1)** on any violation, any sideways scroll, any console or page error, any failed request, and any 4xx or 5xx reply, except the session check's 401 while signed out, the wrong sign-in code, and the two refusals the walk asks for (which must come). `KAINOW_WALK_ONLY=1` stops after the walk, for a quicker check while working on these screens.

It needs no Docker and no AI key. It needs a scratch database and the API running against it:

```
createdb kainow_a11y
export DATABASE_URL=postgres://.../kainow_a11y AUTH_SECRET=<32+ random characters> LOG_SIGN_IN_CODES=true COOKIE_SECURE=false PORT=3066 APP_URL=http://localhost:3066
cd app && npm run build && npm run build:web && npm run migrate
npm run setup-invite -- a11y@example.org "Access Check" > /tmp/a11y/setup.log   # the one-time setup link
node dist/main.js > /tmp/a11y/api.log 2>&1 &        # keep this running
node dist/worker/main.js > /tmp/a11y/worker.log 2>&1 &   # sends the project invitations (their links are read from its log)
curl -s -XPOST localhost:3066/auth/request-code -H 'content-type: application/json' -d '{"email":"a11y@example.org"}'
cd ../tests/a11y && npm install && node audit.mjs
```

The script signs in with the code printed in the API log (which uses the setup link), audits the used setup link, the sign-in page with Kainow's defaults and the onboarding screen empty, then gives the name "Example Organisation", the domain example.org, a department and both logos, audits onboarding filled, finishes setup, and then creates a project (making the administrator its Project Manager and Owner, with a separate Member, directly in the database), invites a Sponsor, disables and re-enables the Member so their membership is On Hold, records an Audit request, issues a handover, creates a Closed project, adds one note, and inserts one finished extraction run with four proposals (a repeated quote, a quote that is not in the notes, an owner and a deadline) so the review screen has something to check. After the other screens it audits the Organisation screen, the sign-in page with the organisation's logos, the header with the wide logo only, and sign-in with the square logo only. The protected key may be mounted (`KAINOW_PROTECTED_KEY_FILE`) or not: with it the credential case is cleaned, without it the case waits for the key. Settings: `KAINOW_URL`, `KAINOW_EMAIL`, `KAINOW_API_LOG`, `KAINOW_WORKER_LOG`, `KAINOW_SETUP_LOG`, `CHROME`, and `DATABASE_URL` (for the inserted run). Never point it at a real database.

## What a clean result means

Run on 7 November 2026: 0 violations across 18 screen states. Run on 8 November 2026 (with projects): 0 violations across 34 screen states, after moving the sign-in form on the invitation page out of a second main landmark. With sub-project 1b's screens: 0 violations across 46 screen states. With sub-project 2a (the Work tab, people's proposals, the Authorise form with subtasks, the task complete and risk accept forms, in an extra "Records access check" project): 0 violations across 58 screen states. After the review fixes (with a task's History open): 0 violations across 60 screen states. With onboarding (the setup link page, sign-in with Kainow's defaults, onboarding empty and filled, the Organisation screen, and the header and sign-in with each logo shape): 0 violations across 76 screen states. With sub-project 2b (the screens listed above): 0 violations across 102 screen states. With sub-project 11a, the interface redesign (run on 24 November 2026): 0 violations across 155 screen states (with the Timeline's bars and the Stages box open), and no state scrolls sideways at 375 px. With sub-project 11a.1 (run on 25 November 2026, with the walk and the browser's own errors counted): 0 violations, 0 browser faults across 296 screen states. With sub-project 11a.2 (run on 26 November 2026, with the boards, the side panel, the dark menu, compact spacing and the keyboard-only check, at both widths in Light and Dark): 0 violations, 0 browser faults across 374 screen states, and no state scrolls sideways at 375 px. (Board edges were then below 3:1; since the final-review fix they use the control border and must reach 3:1.) Fixed on the way: the heading order of the Resource catalogue page and of the Organisation page's quarantine cases, and five reads the server refuses (409) while the organisation is being set up. Checked by hand alongside it at widths from 375 to 1200 px: the top bar no longer pushes the person menu off the page at 700 to 740 px, wide tables (the Work tab's) scroll inside their box between 640 and 1100 px instead of the page, and Skip to content moves focus to the content instead of going to Home.

Automated rules find only a part of the real problems (missing names and labels, colour contrast, bad structure). A clean run does not show the product is accessible. Still to do by hand, as part of the January accessibility audit: keyboard-only use of every screen, focus order and visible focus, a screen reader on the review screen (the marked passage and the repeated-passage chooser), zoom to 200%, and reduced motion. The dark theme is audited on Home, the 11a.1 pages and the project tabs only.
